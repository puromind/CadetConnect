# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Nigeria Police Academy cadets and recent alumni using a private community space on mobile and desktop. They need a safe place to keep up with peers, find useful everyday items, chat, and join interest-based communities.

## Product Purpose

CadetConnect brings cadet community, conversations, announcements, and peer-to-peer commerce into one calm, moderated workspace. Success means a cadet can understand what is happening, find the right person or group, and discover a relevant listing in seconds.

## Positioning

An academy-focused community layer that combines social updates, practical marketplace discovery, and peer support without exposing confidential operational information.

## Operating Context

The MVP is a responsive browser experience used between classes, at home, or on a phone. Content is intentionally generic and community-focused: events, study support, entertainment, and everyday goods.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The foundation includes Feed, Chats, Marketplace, and Communities, with sample data, search/filter controls, verified profile badges, report/block affordances, and responsive navigation. This is a front-end MVP with no live authentication, messaging backend, payments, or confidential academy data.

## Brand Commitments

The name CadetConnect and a clear, respectful, welcoming voice are binding. Avoid operational details, sensitive locations, or claims that imply official academy endorsement.

## Evidence on Hand

No production content, brand assets, or user research were supplied. Sample names, listings, and posts are fictional placeholders for the MVP.

## Product Principles

- Useful before noisy.
- Community trust is visible.
- Everyday cadet life, not operational detail.
- Fast on a phone and easy to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, descriptive labels, and layouts that remain usable at narrow widths.
